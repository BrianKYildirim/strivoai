<objective>
Build the D-06 remote database lane — and make it structurally incapable of passing vacuously.

Two findings drive this plan:
1. **Five `__tests__/history-persistence.test.ts` files are DEAD CODE.** They are remote-gated (`skipRemote()`) AND absent from the `test:ci` allowlist, so they execute in NO lane today. The entire S-22 "History correctness" row of the matrix is unproven. D-06 is what resurrects them.

Output: a fail-loud preflight, a generalized remote helper, and exactly one live copy of each History contract.

Verify this plan's work by invoking `deno test` on the files directly.
