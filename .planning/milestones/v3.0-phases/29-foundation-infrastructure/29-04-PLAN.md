## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-29-IDEM | Tampering / Repudiation | check_and_deduct_merits + merit_transactions | mitigate | Partial UNIQUE index `WHERE source='feature-deduct' AND idempotency_key IS NOT NULL` enforces row-level dedup; RPC catches `unique_violation` (SQLSTATE 23505) and returns the prior `transaction_id`. NFR-07 retry-storm tolerance verified by Plan 29-09 parallel-fire test. |
| T-29-IDEM-FAKE | Spoofing | client-supplied UUID | accept | UUID v4 entropy is 122 bits — collision in practice impossible across realistic populations. | T-29-RPC-PRIV | Elevation of Privilege | new 4-arg overload | mitigate | `SECURITY DEFINER` + `SET search_path = public, pg_temp` + REVOKE ALL FROM public + explicit GRANT to `authenticated, service_role`. |
