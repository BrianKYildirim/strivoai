Acceptance asserts no new file under `supabase/migrations/` and no `apply_migration`/`db push` executed.
- **T-02 (MEDIUM) — Leaking the captured policy detail as a value.** The rollback must faithfully restore the prior over-permissive policy, captured READ-ONLY from `pg_policies` (Source C). **Mitigation:** names-not-values discipline — the policy NAME + the SQL shape are recorded, not any secret/value; the captured prior-policy text is the policy definition (public SQL DDL, not a secret), and the secret-grep guard confirms no key/token/PII leaked.

No HIGH threat left unmitigated: T-01 is closed by embed-not-file + run-nothing + the no-new-migration-file acceptance; T-02 by names-not-values + secret-grep=0.
</threat_model>
