<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Risk | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Browser/authenticated role -> costing tables/views | Forged costs or cross-user disclosure | RLS deny-all plus revoked grants; service-role writes/reads only |
| Current profile/config -> historical record | Retroactive tier, exclusion, or price drift | Snapshot values on the raw event and link effective pricing row |
| Auth deletion -> retained costing history | Re-identification or accidental cascade | Transactional UUID detachment and random non-derived costing subject |
| Retention/cron -> raw ledgers | Premature loss or duplicate aggregation | Idempotent aggregate-before-delete function with fixed cutoffs and action counts |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-04.2-01 | Tampering | Raw ledgers and price catalog | mitigate | Service-role-only writes, append-only pricing, controlled constraints, idempotency keys. |
| T-04.2-02 | Information Disclosure | Costing views | mitigate | Deny anon/authenticated grants and suppress small cohorts. |
| T-04.2-03 | Linkability | Deleted-account history | mitigate | Null Auth UUIDs and retain only random costing subject IDs. |
| T-04.2-04 | Repudiation | Historical cost | mitigate | Snapshot effective pricing ID, exact usage, cost confidence, and complete epoch. |
| T-04.2-05 | Denial of Service | Telemetry indexes/retention | mitigate | Narrow indexes, bounded scheduled batches, idempotent aggregation and pruning. |
</threat_model>
