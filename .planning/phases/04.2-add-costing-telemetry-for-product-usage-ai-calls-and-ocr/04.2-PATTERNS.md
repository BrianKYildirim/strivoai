## Patterns to Avoid

- Do not create a second AI-call table or dual-write path.
- Do not use `analysis_jobs`, `req_id`, or client input as the universal feature-run identity.
- Do not infer production from URL, Vite build mode, or Supabase project naming.
- Do not resolve historical tier, subscription interval, exclusion, model price, or account classification from current rows.
- Do not place raw prompts, outputs, document text, provider bodies, emails, or payment details in telemetry.
- Do not make Merit settlement or product success depend on telemetry persistence.
- Do not add a cascading Auth foreign key to raw costing rows that must survive account deletion.
- Do not count a Claude vision attempt once in AI totals and again as standalone OCR spend.
