<objective>
Close the responsive, touch and motion contract (UI-SPEC Surface F) across the 11 audit surfaces, fixing the defects plan 03-05 reproduced.

Purpose: XDEV-05 and XDEV-09 are where "works on my device" fails most visibly — a 16px input that triggers iOS focus zoom, a 32px tap target, a `100vh` container that hides content behind the iOS URL bar, and a screen running ten concurrent compositor animations on a low-memory phone. All four are measurable and all four are reproduced.
Output: real `dvh` utilities with a guaranteed fallback, compliant form controls and tap targets, an enforced animation budget, and the discovery specs promoted to regression tests.

Scope guard: `UniversalAnalyzing`'s own animation reduction is part of its Surface D rewrite in plan 03-15 — this plan must not edit that component. `index.html`'s `viewport-fit=cover` is plan 03-07's edit; this plan only asserts it.
</objective>

<threat_model>
- **Silent cascade inversion (the subtle one).** `min-h-screen min-h-[100dvh]` looks like a fallback pair but Tailwind emits utilities grouped by property with its own internal sort, so which `min-height` wins is not guaranteed by class order. Shipping that pattern produces a fallback that works on the maintainer's build and inverts on another. Mitigation: one custom utility emitting both declarations in one rule, in source order — the only construction where the cascade is deterministic.
- **Accessibility regression disguised as a fix.** Enlarging tap targets by inflating visuals, or removing pinch-zoom to stop iOS focus zoom, would each trade one defect for a WCAG violation. Mitigation: hit areas are expanded with padding or an `::before` overlay rather than by growing the visual; `user-scalable=no` and `maximum-scale` are explicitly forbidden and asserted absent (WCAG 1.4.4); every icon-only control gains an `aria-label`.
- **Layout change breaking a money path.** Editing shared `ui/` primitives (`textarea`, `select`) touches every form in the app including checkout and onboarding. A wrong Tailwind class could clip a payment field on mobile. Mitigation: the change is the minimal `text-sm` to `text-base md:text-sm` swap that `input.tsx` already ships; the full existing E2E suite plus the five-project responsive spec are the gate.
- **Assertions that cannot see the truth.** `env(safe-area-inset-*)` resolves to `0px` under Playwright emulation and `getComputedStyle` returns the resolved value, so a computed-style safe-area assertion would be a false green. `document.getAnimations()` cannot see framer-motion's RAF transforms, so an animation-count assertion alone would also be a false green. Mitigation: safe-area goes to the static guard plus XTEST-04's manual pass; the motion budget requires BOTH the `getAnimations()` count and a static-source guard that infinite motion is gated behind `useReducedMotion()`.
- **Budget breach.** Eight widths times eleven surfaces times two themes is a large case count on five projects; unchecked it blows the +3 min gate budget and drives `-SkipVerify`. Mitigation: parameterise the case matrix in one place, re-measure after promotion, and cut cases (recorded in the residual register) rather than dropping a project.
- **Token drift.** A quick contrast fix with a literal hex would violate the project's semantic-token rule and the UI-SPEC's color contract. Mitigation: no hardcoded color may be introduced; the only sanctioned hardcoded-color surface in this phase is the ES5 guard in plan 03-07.
</threat_model>
