<threat_model>
ASVS Level 1 scope: irreversible billing-mutation state machine and duplicate financial-mutation prevention.

Threats:
- **T-05 HIGH - duplicate financial mutation after confirmed renewal (ASVS V5 business logic):** a reconciliation rejection after explicit Stripe success rewinds the dialog to restore_error, clears the in-flight guard, and re-exposes Retry, allowing a second `renew-subscription` restore invoke (double provider mutation). Mitigation: guard `runRestore` when `stripeConfirmedRef.current` is already true so no second restore can be dispatched, and never transition to `restore_error` once Stripe is confirmed.
- **T-06 HIGH - confirmed success mislabeled as failure (ASVS V5 / integrity):** treating a post-confirmation reconciliation rejection as a restore failure shows "downgrade still scheduled"/Retry after Stripe already renewed, contradicting the confirmed truth (violates D-20 "never show renewal success from a failed/ambiguous response" in reverse — never show failure after a confirmed success). Mitigation: end the provider-mutation error boundary at explicit Stripe confirmation; a reconciliation rejection is handled like `timed_out`, keeping the persistent `syncing` state and success toast.

Block-on-HIGH: yes. T-05 or T-06 failure blocks completion; no post-confirmation regression to a retryable/duplicate mutation may be waived.
</threat_model>

<objective>
Close CR-03 so once Stripe explicitly confirms a renewal, the dialog can only advance to syncing/confirmed — a bounded-reconciliation rejection can never rewind it to a retryable restore_error or permit a second restore mutation.

Purpose: Restore the D-17/D-18/D-19/D-20 irreversibility contract and eliminate HIGH threats T-05/T-06 that the initial verification reproduced.
Output: Corrected `RenewEliteDialog` error boundary and guard, plus a focused rejection regression test.
</objective>
