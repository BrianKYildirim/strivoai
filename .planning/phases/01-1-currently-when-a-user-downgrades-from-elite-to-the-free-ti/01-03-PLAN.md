<threat_model>
ASVS Level 1 scope: Pricing billing controls, client state transitions, and accessible financial-action presentation.

Threats:
- **T-09 HIGH - duplicate downgrade/renewal action (ASVS V5 business logic):** stale UI or keyboard/programmatic activation could schedule cancellation twice or expose conflicting billing mutations. Mitigation: native disabled Current/Scheduled controls, callback-level guard in `TierCardV2`, a second pending-state guard in `handleTierCta`, and renewal in-flight locks inherited from the shared dialog.
- **T-10 HIGH - stale-state privilege/billing confusion (ASVS V4/V5):** admin tier simulation, inactive subscription status, or delayed webhook data could misrepresent real billing status. Mitigation: warnings/actions use only `useSubscriptionState` and its `active`/`trialing` predicate; Elite entitlement remains current; local provisional/suppression state never writes the profile and only follows an explicit server response.
- **T-11 MEDIUM - misleading financial copy/accessibility (ASVS V5/V14):** missing dates, color-only status, or low-contrast actions could mislead users. Mitigation: exact locked copy, no guessed date/price, semantic text+icon states, native disabled controls, and UI-SPEC contrast/focus rules.

Block-on-HIGH: yes. T-09 or T-10 failure blocks completion; no duplicate billing action or inactive/simulated-state recovery path may be waived.
</threat_model>
