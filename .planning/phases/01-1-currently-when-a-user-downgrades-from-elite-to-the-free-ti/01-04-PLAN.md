<threat_model>
ASVS Level 1 scope: dashboard session state, billing-warning integrity, shared financial action, and final cross-surface regression.

Threats:
- **T-12 HIGH - stale timer/session leakage (ASVS V3 session management):** a timeout or dismissal from one user/period could hide or mutate another user's state. Mitigation: session keys include user+period, storage access is guarded, all one-shot timers clear/re-arm on user/period/pending/local-confirmed changes and unmount, and callbacks re-check current eligibility.
- **T-13 HIGH - false renewal or expired-action exposure (ASVS V5 business logic):** an alert could remain actionable after expiry or disappear after a failed/ambiguous restore. Mitigation: exact-instant predicate, immediate suppression only from explicit validated Stripe confirmation, failure preservation, and shared-dialog HIGH-threat tests from Plans 01-02.
- **T-14 MEDIUM - inaccessible/obscured urgency control (ASVS V14 configuration/UI):** dismiss could overlap content or color-only countdown could hide meaning. Mitigation: text countdown/date, meaningful Alert heading, 40px dismiss target with exact aria-label, reserved spacing, keyboard focus ring, and responsive tests/manual visual checks.

Block-on-HIGH: yes. T-12/T-13 failures or a red T-01/T-02/T-03/T-05/T-06/T-09/T-10 billing/auth/CORS regression blocks phase completion even if the visual component renders.
</threat_model>
