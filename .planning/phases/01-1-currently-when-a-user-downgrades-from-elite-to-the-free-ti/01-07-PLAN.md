<threat_model>
ASVS Level 1 scope: time-driven billing-state accuracy for mounted sessions.

Threats:
- **T-03 MEDIUM - stale time makes a mounted tab misrepresent billing status (ASVS V5 business logic):** `deriveSubscriptionState` samples `new Date()` only at fetch, so a tab mounted before period end keeps a Current/Scheduled pending presentation and renewal action past the exact expiry instant, misstating entitlement. Mitigation: schedule a one-shot exact-boundary invalidation in the hook so the cached derivation re-runs with a fresh `now` at period end, flipping `isPendingDowngrade` to false for every consumer.
- **T-04 LOW - timer misfire (early expiry / leak) (ASVS V5 reliability):** an unclamped or uncleaned timer could expire early, over-fire, or leak across user/state changes. Mitigation: reuse `getSafeTimerDelay` (clamped, non-negative) for scheduling and clear the timeout on unmount, user change, and derived-state change; never expire before the exact instant.

Block-on-HIGH: yes (none HIGH here). T-03 must have a passing fake-time regression before completion.
</threat_model>
