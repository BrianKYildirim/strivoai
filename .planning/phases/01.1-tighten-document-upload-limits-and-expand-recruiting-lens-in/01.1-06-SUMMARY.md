## Deviations from Plan

- The original plan said every known `<Pages>3</Pages>` value must reject. Direct Plan 03 evidence falsified that assumption: Word 16 emitted Pages=3 for a genuine two-page document. The verdict therefore accepts this contradictory metadata when extracted words remain at or below 1,600, and rejects when both signals exceed their limits.
- The Deno test imports the shared DOCX generator directly but inlines the two inert text payloads because the browser-oriented spoof fixture uses extensionless bundler imports that strict Deno cannot resolve.

## Issues Encountered

- Initial Deno checking found the extensionless import in the browser spoof fixture and an ArrayBuffer generic mismatch. Removing that browser-only import and copying bytes into an owned Uint8Array resolved both.
