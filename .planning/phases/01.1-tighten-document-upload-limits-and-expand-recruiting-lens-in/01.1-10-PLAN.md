<objective>
Wire the two validation layers into every real resume flow and implement the approved UI interaction/accessibility contract without redesigning the product.

Purpose: A correct standalone validator does not protect users until every storage-writing path calls it in the right order, and the anonymous demo handoff must be gated while the `File` object still exists.
Output: five authoritative integrations, two anonymous client gates, and executable UI/order guards.
</objective>

<threat_model>
- **Bypass through a missed surface.** The five persisting flows and two hero paths are enumerated and source-guarded. `Jobs.tsx` is deliberately transitive-only under D-29 because its submit endpoint is absent.
- **Partial state.** In every persisting flow, validation occurs after historical dedup reuse but before the first storage/row/analysis/Merit side effect. A rejection returns immediately.
- **Cross-session replay.** Anonymous hero inputs are validated before `sessionStorage`; invalid extracted text cannot later become a real Auth.tsx analysis after signup.
- **Duplicate submission.** Busy state disables the primary CTA, browse/change controls, and drop handler. Event-handler guards enforce the same rule beyond visual disabled state.
- **Accessibility regression.** Rejections clear invalid selection but focus the originating browse control, never the toast. Busy state and descriptions are semantic, not color-only.
- **Billing regression.** Existing paywall/Merit guards remain unchanged and still decide whether a valid file can proceed. Validation itself does not deduct or refund.
</threat_model>
