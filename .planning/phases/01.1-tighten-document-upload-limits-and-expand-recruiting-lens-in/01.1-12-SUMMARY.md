(pre-upgrade record)"* mid-results. Traced read-only:

- The DB is correct — `resumes.analyzed_tier = 'elite'`, written 08:16:21Z by the
  service-role save (`analyze-resume/index.ts:113`).
- The UI is stale. `src/pages/Results.tsx:258-262` merges only `overall_score` and
  `grading_data` into local `resume` state on stream completion. `analyzed_tier` is never

  `EliteRerunCta` (`src/components/EliteRerunCta.tsx:38`) takes its unknown-tier branch.

Impact: every fresh streaming analysis by an Elite user shows the "unrecorded tier" line
until reload. Cosmetic/trust, not data corruption — no wrong charge, since the unknown
branch is deliberately non-charging.
