    :624 quality-floor retry). This matters: the :472 retry runs at a DOUBLED effective cap
    (`Math.min(baseMaxTokens*2, 12000)`), so without the attempt field a retry row would be averaged in
    with first-attempt rows and skew both the peak and the headroom (Pitfall 5).

    path; `free-single` on both `executeFreeAnalysis` sites. For the four legacy/fallback sites
    (:714, :1006-1011, :1591-1596, :1732-1737) use a descriptive callName prefixed `legacy-` so a
    stray row is instantly identifiable in the report — RESEARCH expects zero rows from these.
